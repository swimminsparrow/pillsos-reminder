Full Description:
💊 <b>PillSOS — Medication reminder with voice alarm, SOS, and fall detection.</b>

Need to remember a medication or therapy routine? 
Want to be sure a loved one took their medicine on time?
Need an alert if an elderly or fragile relative suffers a fall? 
<b>PillSOS</b> is the answer you are looking for.

───────────────
✨ <b>WHY IS PILLSOS DIFFERENT?</b>
───────────────

🔔 <b>CLEAR & LOUD ALARMS</b>
Never miss a dose again! The alarm rings at maximum volume and a voice speaks the name of the medicine to take.

🚨 <b>AUTOMATIC MISSED DOSE SOS</b>
Connect with a loved one, family member, or friend. If the person doesn't confirm taking the pill, you will receive an automatic SOS when the alarm expires.

⚠️ <b>FALL DETECTION</b>
If the phone detects a fall, it sends a request for help to connected contacts.

👨‍👩‍👧 <b>DESIGNED FOR CAREGIVERS AND FAMILIES</b>
The practical solution for the assistance and peace of mind of parents, grandparents, or loved ones living alone.

🤝 <b>CONNECT WITH ANYONE IN 5 SECONDS</b>
No Account: just a simple 6-digit code to connect with your loved ones.

💚 <b>THE "I'M OK" BUTTON</b>
Send a good morning whenever you want by saying "I'm OK" to all your loved ones.

🌍 <b>MULTI-LANGUAGE & DIALECTS</b>
Translated into over 20 languages, including Neapolitan, Sicilian, and Romanesco, to always feel right at home.

🔒 <b>TOTAL PRIVACY</b>
No email, no password. Your reminders are stored offline on your phone. Messages exchanged over the optional SOS network are end-to-end encrypted.
─────────────────────
ℹ️ <i>PillSOS is a reminder and safety support app for daily wellness; it is not a medical device and does not replace official emergency services under any circumstances.</i>
