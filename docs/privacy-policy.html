1. Overview & Core Philosophy

PillSOS is designed with a strict Privacy-by-Design philosophy. Unlike traditional health and reminder applications, PillSOS does not maintain user databases, does not track your physical movements, and does not require an account or registration.

We believe that sensitive medical and personal safety data belongs solely to you and the people you choose to trust.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media accounts.
  • No Location Tracking: The application does not request or access GPS location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).
  • No Telemetry or Behavioral Profiling: We do not log, track, or sell your app interactions, browsing habits, or alarm frequencies.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All application operational data is stored locally in your device's private SQLite database and encrypted preferences:

  • Medication Reminders: Drug names, treatment dosages, reminder times, and treatment durations.
  • Local Pill Photos: Photos taken to help identify pill packages or shapes remain entirely in your device's private sandbox.
  • SOS Contacts: Nicknames, profile thumbnails, and cryptographic public topic IDs of family members or caregivers you explicitly pair with.
  • Alarm History: Timestamps of alarms taken, snoozed, or missed.
Full User Control: You can edit or delete any alarm, contact, or history entry at any time. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local databases and keys.

4. Private SOS Emergency Network & End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

PillSOS includes an emergency SOS network allowing paired devices to send and receive high-priority alerts and "I'm OK" status updates.

  • Client-Side Cryptography (AES-256): All outgoing SOS emergency alerts and status messages are encrypted on your device using AES-256 with a unique symmetric key before ever being transmitted over the network.
  • Mutual PIN Handshake: Pairing between two devices requires entering a temporary 6-digit PIN and requires explicit confirmation (approval) by the recipient. No unverified connections are permitted.
  • Zero Server Access to Content: Even when relayed across the internet, the alert messages are encrypted ciphertext. Nobody—including the relay server operator—has the technical capability to decrypt or read your alerts.

5. Network Relay Infrastructure (ntfy.sh)

To deliver real-time emergency notifications between devices without maintaining proprietary centralized servers, the app uses lightweight pub/sub push relays (by default, ntfy.sh, with support for self-hosted instances).

  • EU Data Residency & GDPR: The public relay server at ntfy.sh is operated and hosted in Germany (European Union, Hetzner Online GmbH), fully compliant with European Union data localization and GDPR privacy standards.
  • Zero-Knowledge Transport: Because message payloads are encrypted client-side using AES-GCM prior to transmission, the relay server acts purely as a zero-knowledge encrypted pipe and has no technical means to inspect, access, or decrypt alert contents.
  • Technical Connection Metadata: When transmitting encrypted alerts, standard technical metadata (device IP address, timestamp, HTTP request headers) is processed by the relay server solely as necessary for TCP/IP network routing.
  • Pseudonymous Channel Names: Channels are addressed via cryptographically random UUIDs (128 bits of entropy) rather than human names, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • Short Data Retention (TTL): Alerts and pairing packets are configured with a transient Time-to-Live (TTL) between 1 minute and 12 hours, after which they are automatically purged by the relay broker.
  • Self-Hosting Support: Users and organizations seeking total data sovereignty may specify their own private self-hosted ntfy server URL in the application settings.

6. Sensor Data & Automatic Fall Detection

PillSOS provides an optional Fall Detection feature to protect elderly or vulnerable individuals:

  • Accelerometer Access: The app utilizes the device's accelerometer sensor in the background to monitor rapid deceleration and impact patterns indicative of a physical fall.
  • Real-Time In-Memory Processing: Raw accelerometer sensor readings are processed locally in volatile device memory and are never stored, logged, or uploaded to any server.

7. Medical & Life-Safety Disclaimer (Non-Medical Device)

PillSOS is a personal reminder and peer notification utility, NOT a certified medical device.

  • Not a Medical Device (EU MDR 2017/745): PillSOS is not registered, evaluated, or certified as a medical device or diagnostic instrument under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 or FDA guidelines. It is designed solely as a personal schedule and memory support aid.
  • No Substitute for Emergency Services: The mutual SOS network and automated fall detection features are best-effort peer-to-peer notification tools. They do not dispatch or connect to public emergency services (such as 112, 999, or 911) and do not guarantee emergency response. In life-threatening emergencies, always dial official emergency services directly.
  • Delivery Disclaimer: Notification delivery depends on mobile carrier data, battery optimization policies, Wi-Fi connectivity, and third-party relay availability. The developer cannot guarantee instantaneous or uninterrupted message delivery.

8. Device Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM To trigger medication alarms at the exact minute, even during Android Doze mode.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To display alarm alerts, ongoing protection status, and emergency SOS notifications.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE To maintain active fall detection and receive emergency alerts when the app is in the background.
VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK To alert the user during high-priority emergencies and wake the screen for alarms.
CAMERA / Media Access Optional: To allow photographing pill packages or setting a contact avatar thumbnail.

9. Distribution Flavors & Advertising Disclosures

PillSOS is published in two distribution builds:

  • F-Droid / FOSS Flavor (free): 100% Free and Open Source Software. Contains zero proprietary Google libraries, zero advertisements, and zero trackers.
  • Google Play Flavor (nonfree): Includes optional Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support continuous open-source maintenance. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and performance diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

10. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

  • Deleting Data: You can delete any contact, alarm, or history log directly within the app settings.
  • Revoking SOS Pairs: Tapping "Disconnect" immediately removes the peer's topic and cryptographic keys from your device.
  • App Removal: Completely uninstalling PillSOS permanently removes all local SQLite databases, encryption keys, and SharedPreferences from your device.
